## English

### In short

TEOS IT does not collect, store or sell your personal data. Agiorit has no user account, ads, analytics or tracking. The calendar and prayers are part of the app and work without internet.

### Data on your device

The settings you choose (language, local church, calendar, text size), your saved prayers, family patron saint, commemoration lists and reminders are stored only on your device. We have no access to this data.

Deleting the app permanently deletes all of it.

### Location

The map of churches and monasteries may ask for access to your location to show churches near you. Your location is used only on the device while you use the map; it is not sent to us and not stored. The place name is determined by the system geocoder (Apple or Google).

You can turn location access on or off in your device settings. The rest of the app works without it.

### Reminders

Reminders for prayers, fasts and feasts are local notifications scheduled by the device itself. We do not send them through a server and do not collect any data about them.

### Purchases

Supporting development is voluntary and done through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.

To verify purchases we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and transaction details, but not your name or email address.

### Third-party services

The app connects to the internet only for the map and purchases. Map tiles are provided by OpenFreeMap ([openfreemap.org](https://openfreemap.org)), and purchases are handled by Apple, Google and RevenueCat. Their privacy policies apply to that data:

- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

Church data comes from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL license). The app downloads it per country from our server and stores it on the device; your location is not sent.

### Children

Children can use Agiorit safely, because the app does not collect personal data from anyone.

### Your rights

Since we do not store your personal data, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. For questions about data handled by Apple, Google or RevenueCat, contact us and we will help.

### Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version in the app and on this page with the date of the last update.

### Contact

Send privacy questions to [contact@teosit.com](mailto:contact@teosit.com).

## Srpski

### Ukratko

TEOS IT ne prikuplja, ne čuva i ne prodaje vaše lične podatke. Agiorit nema korisnički nalog, reklame, analitiku ni praćenje. Kalendar i molitve su deo aplikacije i rade bez interneta.

### Podaci na vašem uređaju

Podešavanja koja izaberete (jezik, pomesna crkva, kalendar, veličina slova), sačuvane molitve, krsna slava, pomjanik i podsetnici čuvaju se samo na vašem uređaju. Mi nemamo pristup tim podacima.

Brisanjem aplikacije svi ovi podaci se trajno brišu.

### Lokacija

Mapa hramova i manastira može da zatraži dozvolu za pristup lokaciji, da bi prikazala hramove u vašoj blizini. Lokacija se koristi samo na uređaju dok koristite mapu, ne šalje se nama i ne čuva se. Naziv mesta određuje geokoder sistema (Apple ili Google).

Pristup lokaciji možete uključiti ili isključiti u podešavanjima uređaja. Ostatak aplikacije radi i bez njega.

### Podsetnici

Podsetnici na molitve, postove i praznike su lokalna obaveštenja koja zakazuje sam uređaj. Ne šaljemo ih preko servera i ne prikupljamo podatke o njima.

### Kupovine

Podrška razvoju je dobrovoljna i obavlja se preko App Store-a ili Google Play-a. Plaćanje obrađuju Apple ili Google, a mi ne vidimo podatke o vašoj kartici.

Za proveru kupovina koristimo uslugu RevenueCat, koja dobija anonimni identifikator i podatke o transakciji, ali ne i vaše ime ili email adresu.

### Usluge trećih strana

Aplikacija se povezuje na internet samo za mapu i kupovine. Podlogu mape isporučuje OpenFreeMap ([openfreemap.org](https://openfreemap.org)), a kupovine obrađuju Apple, Google i RevenueCat. Na te podatke primenjuju se njihove politike privatnosti:

- [Apple – politika privatnosti](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Google – politika privatnosti](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [RevenueCat – politika privatnosti](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

Podaci o hramovima potiču iz OpenStreetMap-a (© OpenStreetMap contributors, licenca ODbL). Aplikacija ih preuzima po zemljama sa našeg servera i čuva na uređaju; pri tome se ne šalje vaša lokacija.

### Deca

Agiorit mogu bezbedno koristiti i deca, jer aplikacija ni od koga ne prikuplja lične podatke.

### Vaša prava

Pošto ne čuvamo vaše lične podatke, nemamo šta da vam pokažemo, ispravimo ili obrišemo. Za pitanja o podacima koje obrađuju Apple, Google ili RevenueCat obratite nam se i pomoći ćemo vam.

### Izmene

Ako promenimo ovu politiku, objavićemo novu verziju u aplikaciji i na ovoj stranici i navesti datum poslednje izmene.

### Kontakt

Pitanja o privatnosti pošaljite na [contact@teosit.com](mailto:contact@teosit.com).
